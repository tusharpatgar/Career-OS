## 4. Discovery interview plan

Interview [TBD] people who recently experienced the problem. Ask about past behaviour, not hypothetical enthusiasm.

Suggested questions:
- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- What triggered it?
- What did you do, step by step?
- What was the hardest or most expensive part?
- What have you already tried?
- Who else was involved in the decision?
- What happens if you do nothing?
- How often does this occur?

Do not pitch the solution during the first part of the interview.

## 8. Go / no-go gate

Proceed to a build only when:
- Multiple target users independently describe the same concrete problem.
- The problem has a meaningful cost or consequence.
- A reachable initial segment is identified.
- A test supports the proposed value—not just polite interest.
- The first workflow can be delivered safely and measured.
